06 · Design System · Direction A

Surveyed Ground

Atmosphere is the ground. Evidence is the mark. The two never occupy the same layer. This page renders the build rules on the system's own palette — dark near-black ground, bone text, one ember accent for evidence, one moss accent for interaction, hairline borders, and no glow.

Derived from 03-visual-direction.md and 04-voice-and-copy.md. Invents no new visual direction and no new copy. Companion to docs/design.md. Font stacks fall back to system faces until public/fonts (Clash Display · Satoshi · IBM Plex Mono) is created.

02 · The two accents — fixed jobs

Ember marks evidence. Moss marks interaction.

Exactly two accents, one job each, one accent per section. Colour never carries meaning alone — every accent state ships with a text or border change beside it. Derive flagship is ember territory; the jigsaw is moss territory. Cold blue and yellow are dropped.

--ember#C8A95Bverified numerals · evidence · focus rings
--moss#9CAA69hover · active nav tick · selected parcel
--moss-deep#4E5A44pressed / active only

Evidence example — tabular numerals, ember on the verified number only: 15,000 → 40,000+ members through the TGE period.

Moss-on-ground is the riskiest pairing — contrast-test before approval. If it fails 4.5:1 for text, moss is borders/ticks only and text uses bone.

04 · Two image classes

Every image is Terrain or a Plate

COMMUNITY · 03

Terrain — bounded, grained, desaturated atmosphere. Never behind text. Full bleed only for the jigsaw field and case-page route imagery.

ARTEFACT PLATE

Traders Breakfast, Coinfest Bali · Derive's first live event, 100+ attendees · produced

Plates: 1px hairline border, mandatory mono caption beneath on solid ground, native aspect, lazy-load against a solid placeholder. Never more than three plates per viewport. Text never sits on imagery.

05 · Caption schema

Evidence appears only as annotation

TypeStructure
Evidenceproject · artefact or figure · Desi's verb — ember on verified number only
Eventname · place · attendance if verified · role verb — no atmosphere adjectives
Artefactwhat it is · what it was for · Desi's contribution — under 15 words
Confidentialname the domain only — never clients, targets, pipelines, terms
Early-stagethe question being tested — never an outcome that doesn't exist

No KPI cards. No floating figures. One proof per artefact. No counting animation on numerals. If a number can't travel with its artefact and caption, it doesn't appear.

06 · Jigsaw — the one signature interaction

A completed, surveyed field

Seven irregular parcels cut from one continuous terrain photo by 1px survey lines. No interlocking tabs. Complete from the first frame — distinguished by seam, tone, and texture, never absence. Appears once; moss territory. Parcels are real buttons in DOM order with a 2px moss focus ring; arrow keys move between them; capsule/bottom-sheet traps focus and closes on Escape.

07 · Section rules

Derive flagship

Densest section, deliberately. Ember territory. One large plate + two stacked plates, captions beneath carry the numbers. ~3× the Now section. Plates lift 2px on hover with a border brighten — nothing else.

Now ledger

Three rows — HUNCHR, Lemon Tree, Tago. No images, no metrics. Status tags only. Verbs of testing, never achievement. Restraint is the honesty. HUNCHR row ~1.3×.

Work hierarchy

Narrative weight, not chronology. Tiers: Flagship · Current · Selected · Foundations (never "Archive"). RMIT never adjacent to Derive metrics. Recruiter experience index up top.

About

Only personal-first page. Portrait as a bounded plate, marks in the rail, no glowing pill. Essay at 65ch. The jigsaw explained once, here. "Systems of Belonging" survives as one phrase.

08 · Motion

Settling, not performing

  • Page entry: fade up 8px, staggered by section, once.
  • The only scroll-linked effect sitewide: jigsaw parcels gaining definition on entry.
  • Capsule: 250ms fade + 8px rise. Hover: 150ms border/label brighten. Routes: 150ms fade.
  • Reduced motion: everything instant; parcels render fully defined.

Static under all conditions: hero, all body text, all captions, the footer statement.

09 · Global bans

  • No glow, anywhere. No text on imagery. No third accent.
  • No KPI cards. No floating figures. No counting/odometer numerals.
  • No childish puzzle tabs. No interlocking pieces. No repeated jigsaw explanation.
  • No neon / generic-crypto styling. No per-word text animation. Nothing below 12px.
  • No invented copy — escalate instead. Retired vocabulary stays retired.
