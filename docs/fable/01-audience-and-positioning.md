## Full approved content

### 1. Audience

**Primary audience.** Early-stage founders and team leads across Web3, AI, consumer products, social products, sports and entertainment, and emerging technology. Visitors may arrive cold (Twitter, LinkedIn, search, forwarded link) or warm (event, introduction, professional relationship, existing network). At the company stage most likely to hire Desi, the founder may also function as the hiring manager. The more useful distinction is therefore cold visitors (who require immediate clarity) versus warm visitors (who already know Desi and want greater depth). Cold visitors set the clarity requirement. Warm visitors set the depth requirement.

**Secondary audience.** Hiring managers and recruiters looking for cross-functional experience across growth, community, partnerships, ecosystem, events, business development, early-stage distribution, and experience strategy.

**Tertiary audience.** Creative, event, community, sports, and strategic collaborators.

**Lowest-priority audience.** Creative peers visiting primarily for aesthetic inspiration. The portfolio should still earn their respect, but it should not optimise for them at the expense of professional clarity.

**Consequences.** The homepage should be designed for a founder who may initially skim for approximately 60–90 seconds. Recruiters should be supported through immediate role legibility, accurate dates, clear role history, visible outcomes, a scannable Work page, and straightforward contact information. The portfolio does not need to imitate a conventional case-study grid or résumé website. It must remain distinctive, editorial, authored, and emotionally intelligent.

### 2. Ten-Second Understanding

Within approximately 10 seconds, a first-time visitor should understand: Desi's name; that she works across growth, community, and partnerships; that she is suited to early-stage teams; that she has worked across Web3 and is moving toward broader consumer, social, AI, and sports-related environments; one strong piece of professional evidence; that she is open to the right role or collaboration; that the portfolio itself demonstrates strong visual and cultural judgment.

The strongest current evidence fragment: Derive Discord growth from approximately 15,000 to more than 40,000 members through the TGE period.

### 3. Core Career Throughline — "Participation engineering"

This is an internal framing term. It does not have to appear publicly on the website.

Desi's repeatable capability is designing the conditions under which strangers become participants, participants become communities, communities create momentum, and relationships create commercial value.

She repeatedly observes the behavioural friction and environmental cues that others may treat as background. Examples include: why the layout of a room prevents conversation; why an onboarding step causes people to leave; why a community becomes quiet after a launch or airdrop; why a customer hesitates during a high-consideration purchase; why a group becomes active around a match, event, campaign, or shared ritual; why people observe but do not participate; why people participate once but do not return.

She reads physical and digital environments as behavioural systems.

**What Desi repeatedly creates.** Entry points, events, rituals, campaigns, invitations, referrals, partnership structures, communications, community systems, reasons to participate, reasons to return, reasons to bring others.

**What Desi repeatedly improves.** Attention into participation. Participation into community. Community into growth. Relationships into partnerships. Trust into commercial outcomes. Isolated activity into repeat behaviour.

**Origin of the capability.**

- *RMIT.* Spatial design developed Desi's understanding that environments influence behaviour deliberately.
- *Misura and early events.* Trust-building, client awareness, hosting, commercial communication, high-consideration decision support, understanding of physical and social environments.
- *Derive.* Proved the capability at digital scale across community, campaigns, events, partnerships, brand, product communication, launch environments, and ecosystem coordination.
- *Lemon Tree and Tago.* Extend the capability into business development, partnerships, commercial positioning, prospect and market development, emerging technology.
- *HUNCHR.* Beginning to test the capability through product distribution and social mechanics: private leagues, competition, referrals, group identity, banter, invitation behaviour, community-led acquisition.

**Commercial usefulness.** Early-stage teams can purchase impressions or attention, but cannot easily purchase trust, belonging, contribution, repeat participation, genuine advocacy, or durable relationships. Desi's capability is commercially useful because it connects human behaviour, community, communication, events, partnerships, and growth. It differs from generic community work (which may focus on maintaining a space). It differs from generic growth work (which may focus on optimising an existing funnel). Desi's work focuses on why the space, experience, relationship, or funnel becomes meaningful enough to participate in at all.

### 4. Approved Positioning Territory — Growth Through Gathering

**Practical professional identity.** Growth, community, and partnerships operator for early-stage teams. Cross-functional early-stage profile capable of working across community, ecosystem, events, campaigns, partnerships, launch communication, early-stage distribution, relationship development, content and communication.

**Distinctive value proposition.** Desi creates earned participation as a growth engine. She builds the communities, events, partnerships, communications, and participation systems that help people show up, care, stay, contribute, return, and bring others. She connects this to growth, retention, ecosystem momentum, partnerships, distribution, and commercial outcomes.

**Evidence.** Strongest current evidence is Derive. Support from RMIT, RISA, Misura, Bruce Media, Lemon Tree, Tago, and current HUNCHR work.

**Opportunities.** Early growth roles; community leadership; ecosystem roles; partnerships; business development; community-led growth; event and experience strategy; launch and campaign work; cross-functional first-ten-hire roles.

Many candidates may claim similar functions. Portfolio must differentiate through the spatial-design origin, the participation thesis, specific evidence, strong writing, the jigsaw system, visual craft, and the connection between physical and digital environments. The identity may also understate Desi's design background if About and visual direction do not preserve it.

**Longevity.** Grows toward Head of Growth, Head of Community, Head of Ecosystem, Head of Partnerships, Growth and Partnerships Lead, Community-Led Growth Lead, cross-functional early-stage operator.

### 5. Supporting Future Direction — Product-Led Participation

Visible direction of travel, not the primary proof.

Focuses on growth designed into a product's social and behavioural mechanics: group identity, social competition, referrals, leagues, invitations, shareable moments, community loops, participation systems, product-led distribution.

Most clearly connected to HUNCHR. May also draw from Derive's community and launch work, Bruce Media's sports experience, events, community behaviour, and referral/participation mechanics.

**Current limitation.** HUNCHR is still new and does not yet have enough verified public outcomes. Using it as the primary identity today would place the portfolio's strongest claim ahead of its evidence.

**Current role in the portfolio.** Should influence vocabulary, future-direction framing, the Now section, About-page direction, how HUNCHR is described, and the types of opportunities the portfolio remains open to. Should not currently carry primary proof weight.

**Future potential.** May become more central if HUNCHR or future consumer-product work produces verified evidence across activation, referrals, retention, group participation, consumer growth, product-led community, or social distribution. Reassess this positioning when meaningful outcomes exist.

### 6. Architecture of Belonging

Role in strategy: Desi's origin story, her design intelligence, the conceptual foundation underneath her work. Part of the About narrative. Part of the visual and spatial language of the site.

**Why not primary.** As primary identity, risks positioning Desi too narrowly as a community designer, experience designer, events specialist, or culture-focused creative. Would undersell growth, business development, partnerships, commercial range, emerging technology, product distribution, and her current direction.

**What should survive.** The RMIT origin; spatial thinking; editorial composition; the site's grid and structure; the understanding that environments shape behaviour; the concept of systems of belonging; the emotional intelligence of the portfolio; the About page.

This territory should make Growth Through Gathering feel distinctive rather than replacing it.

### 7. Brand Thesis — *I design the conditions for people to find each other.*

Strongest poetic and conceptual thesis. Active. First-person. Specific enough to feel authored. Broad enough to connect different roles. Relevant to physical and digital environments. Flexible enough to grow with Desi's career.

Applies across spatial design, events, community, growth, partnerships, Web3, sports, social products, consumer behaviour.

The thesis should provide emotional and conceptual meaning. It should not be expected to explain Desi's practical role by itself. Professional clarity and poetic identity should support each other rather than competing.

**Supporting ideas.**

- *Designing why people gather, care, stay, and return.* Use as section logic, supporting editorial language, a way to organise deeper thinking. Do not necessarily use as the main headline.
- *Systems of Belonging.* Use selectively — an About-page idea, essay or section title, a conceptual layer, a visual or editorial phrase. Not the only positioning (feels static/abstract without practical framing).
- *Community as part of the product.* Strategic belief. Connected to HUNCHR and future product work. Editorial or social-content concept. Supporting point inside case studies. Not currently the primary brand thesis.

**Retired.** *Growth as emotional architecture.* Removed from primary positioning system. Risks sounding overly abstract and AI-generated.

### 8. HUNCHR's Strategic Role

Supporting signal. Should influence Desi's future direction without carrying proof it has not earned.

**Signals.** Interest in consumer and social products; movement toward sports and entertainment; product-led community; early-stage distribution; social competition; referral mechanics; user behaviour; community-informed product strategy.

**Currently proves.** Only the work Desi is actively doing: distribution experiments; user conversations; community outreach; invitation messaging; referral thinking; identifying onboarding friction; partnership exploration; football-community outreach; event-led acquisition ideas. Do not present unverified outcomes.

**Portfolio treatment now.** A current chapter. A direction signal. Honest early-stage work. An application of Desi's existing practice to a consumer social product. NOT: flagship status, invented outcomes, fabricated traction, inflated metrics, or a full success narrative without evidence.

**Future reassessment.** If meaningful outcomes become available, HUNCHR may later support a stronger positioning around consumer growth, product-led participation, sports and entertainment, or social-product distribution.

### 9. Jigsaw Direction — Consolidate and mature

The jigsaw remains the portfolio's signature conceptual spine. It should not remain a repeated verbal theme across every section. It should also not be demoted into a meaningless decorative motif.

**Strategic purpose.** Demonstrates that Desi's apparently fragmented roles form one connected practice. Invert the current agency of the metaphor: Desi is not an unfinished puzzle. Desi is the person who sees how different pieces connect and assembles them into a working system.

**Implementation principles.** One exceptional jigsaw interaction. Every piece connected to real work, capability, or evidence. Remove repetitive verbal explanations. Avoid puzzle terminology in every section title. Eliminate accidental unfinished states. Do not use missing pieces as the primary symbol. Do not present Desi as incomplete. Avoid childish puzzle styling. Avoid invisible hover-only interactions. Ensure touch, keyboard, and mobile use. Make interaction lead somewhere meaningful. Allow the system to grow when new career chapters appear.

**Role.** Supports career coherence, memorability, evidence navigation, the relationship between different chapters, and the spatial-design origin. Should not replace professional positioning, information architecture, clear project hierarchy, evidence, or navigation.

### 12. Gains and Trade-offs

**Gains.** Immediate professional legibility; credible alignment with current evidence; relevance to actual early-stage roles; room to move into consumer and social products later; strong relationship between atmosphere and evidence; preservation of design intelligence; a clear role for the jigsaw; a portfolio capable of attracting founders, recruiters, and collaborators.

**Gives up.** Leading publicly with the more romantic identity of community and experience designer; making spatial design the headline; presenting HUNCHR as more mature than it is; using the newest role as the central proof; allowing the jigsaw metaphor to explain everything; optimising the portfolio primarily for creative peers.

**Misunderstandings to prevent.** The portfolio must prevent visitors from assuming Desi is *only* a community manager, *only* a Web3 operator, *only* a BD person, *only* a visual designer, a generalist with no clear capability, someone claiming consumer-product wins she has not achieved, or someone still trying to assemble an identity. The range is intentional and connected by a repeatable professional capability.
