## Key rules

- **Every number needs context.** Numerals are meaningless without the artefact, the campaign, and the role attached.
- **Ember accent on verified numbers only.**
- **Mono, tabular numerals for all evidence figures.**
- **One proof per artefact.** No stacking.
- **Confidential work:** name the domain. Never expose clients, targets, pipelines, or financial details.
- **Early-stage work:** describe the *question being tested*. Do not claim outcomes before they exist.
- **Artefact captions:** what it is, what it was for, Desi's contribution — under 15 words where possible.
- **Event captions:** name, place, attendance if verified, role verb. No atmosphere adjectives.
- **No exclamation marks.**
- **No sentences beginning with "I believe."**
- **No "let's connect."**
- **Ration these words carefully:** *people, community, belonging, systems, gathering.* Overuse dilutes them.
- **Do NOT let implementation invent new copy.** Use this document.
